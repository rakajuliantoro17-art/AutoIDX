# 1. Project Vision

AURA Trade OS adalah platform trading cryptocurrency modular yang dirancang sebagai **Trading Operating System**, bukan sekadar trading bot.

Sistem dirancang untuk memiliki kemampuan:

- realtime market intelligence
- technical analysis
- strategy execution
- risk management
- paper trading
- backtesting
- live trading
- portfolio management
- analytics
- event-driven architecture
- persistence
- recovery
- reconciliation
- observability
- safety controls
- AI assistance
- machine learning
- multi-exchange support
- automated cloud execution

Tujuan akhir adalah membangun sistem trading yang:

- modular
- deterministic
- observable
- recoverable
- testable
- fault tolerant
- risk controlled
- AI-ready
- scalable
